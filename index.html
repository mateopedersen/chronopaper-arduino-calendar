ChronoPaper

An e-paper calendar design with a date-only Gregorian engine, optional RTC/NTP time and a month-aware printable companion.

Work in progress. The software compiles for Nano ESP32; physical assembly and panel testing are not yet verified. The cover image is a concept illustration.
